    Roadmap to Platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). Work in three stages: story first, then the 100% checklist, then the Strangers & Freaks cleanup. Keep cheats off the whole run — using one disables trophies and saving.

    Stage 1 — Story (about 60–80 hours)

    Play the story end to end. All 12 story trophies pop automatically, along with the mission trophies for Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown and Operation Savage Glory. Do the one missable trophy — Positive Mental Attitude, the Chapter 1 meditation — before Chapter 1 ends. See the Missables tab.

    Yields: all 12 story trophies, plus Positive Mental Attitude.

    Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks cleanup (about 10 hours)

    Finish any remaining Strangers & Freaks strands — Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete — and mop up the last crime and exploration trophies. When the 8th strand is done, the silver pops, and with it the platinum.

    Yields: Strangers & Freaks (silver), then Vice Got a Price! (platinum).

    Missables

    Good news: there is only one trophy you can miss. Everything else can be finished after the story, in free roam.

    Positive Mental Attitude Possibly missable

    What: Meditate at every spot across Leonida — 18 spots in 10 locations.

    Why it can be missed: the first meditation happens at Jason's house in Chapter 1. After the mission “Down to Earth”, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose “Accept”. That spot is gone after Chapter 1.

    Then: meditate at the other 17 spots; you can meditate with both characters at each spot for +2.

    PowerPyx and GTABase flag this one “possibly missable”, not confirmed — do it in Chapter 1 to be safe.
